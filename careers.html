Careers

Build the physical brain of a robot.

Substrate was founded on the belief that a future where robots have true embodied intelligence is fundamentally more exciting than one where they are puppets controlled remotely by datacenters. This requires a specialized NPU that can run a robot’s world model on board, in real time, on 40 watts.

We are looking for exceptional people to build it with us. These are among the hardest problems in computing, and the first few hires will shape the company. We hire for ability and drive over pedigree, and we expect everyone to own more than their title and argue from measurement rather than opinion. The whitepaper is the technical case, and the plan is how we spend against it.
